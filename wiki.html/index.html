Barcelona is a city on the northeastern coast of Spain. It is the capital and largest city of the autonomous community of Catalonia and the second-largest city in Spain.

The city is located on the Mediterranean Sea and is known for its architecture, art, culture, food and beaches.

History

Barcelona was founded as a Roman settlement known as Barcino. It later became an important Mediterranean trading centre.

During the Middle Ages, Barcelona became an important political and economic centre of the Crown of Aragon.

In the nineteenth century, the city expanded through the Eixample district and grew as an industrial and cultural centre.

Geography

Barcelona lies between the Mediterranean Sea and the Collserola mountain range. The city has beaches, parks and densely built urban areas.

Culture

Barcelona has a rich cultural identity. Catalan and Spanish are widely spoken, and the city hosts museums, theatres, festivals and music events.

Tourism

Barcelona is one of Europe's most visited cities. Popular landmarks include the Sagrada Família, Park Güell, La Rambla and the Gothic Quarter.

Architecture

The city is famous for the works of Antoni Gaudí, including the Sagrada Família, Casa Batlló and Casa Milà.

Sports

Football is very popular in Barcelona. FC Barcelona is one of the most successful football clubs in the world.

Economy

Barcelona has a strong economy based on tourism, trade, technology, design, education and finance.

Transport

The city has metro lines, buses, trams and railway services. Barcelona–El Prat Airport connects the city with destinations worldwide.

Education

Barcelona has several universities, research centres and international schools.
